An island made with the **3D terrain** extension: hills and beaches generated from a relief, a dirt path painted with events, the **3D water** around it and **3D grass** swaying in the wind, pushed aside by the player.

Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its layers (grass, dirt, rock, sand). The character walks on the slopes thanks to the 3D physics behavior of the terrain.

The ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt, CC0).
