# 3D car touch controls

Two ways to drive a 3D car on a touchscreen: a floating joystick or a steering wheel to steer, with gas and brake pedals.

The car has the "3D car multitouch mapper" behavior, which reads the sticks of the multitouch controller: the primary stick steers and the secondary stick accelerates or brakes. No event drives the car:
- The **floating joystick** appears where the left side of the screen is touched, and follows the finger.
- The **steering wheel** is turned with the finger (switch to it with the text at the top right, or the C key).
- The **gas and brake pedals** push the secondary stick up or down while they are pressed.

The keyboard (arrows) still works too.
