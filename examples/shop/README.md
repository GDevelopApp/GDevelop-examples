# Shop

A coin clicker with three shops: upgrades with levels, a locked upgrade, skins to equip, items bought with gems, daily deals and a market of 100 stickers. The money and the purchases are saved.

The shops are made with the Shop extension:
- the items are added with actions (or from a variable for the 100 stickers),
- the shop takes the money, increases the levels and equips the skins,
- the events use the conditions "An item was bought" and "A purchase failed" to apply the upgrades and tell the player.
