# 3D Driving Expanded

A 3D car driving game with a third-person camera view, on a large map made with a 3D terrain: a road circuit through the countryside and a village, climbing a hill, with a shortcut over ramps, forests and mountains around. The player accelerates and steers using keyboard or mobile controls. The car uses physics-based movement and can collide with other physics obstacles in the 3D environment.
