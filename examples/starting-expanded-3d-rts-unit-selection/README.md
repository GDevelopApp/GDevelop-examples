# Top-down RTS (unit selection) Expanded

A 3D angled top-down RTS-style game in a valley made with a 3D terrain, with hills, a stream, forests, farms and a town. The player can click to select individual units or click and drag to select multiple units. Selected units can be given a waypoint to move to, using navmesh pathfinding to walk on the terrain and around obstacles, like buildings, trees and the steep sides of the hills, without bumping into each other. The stream is shallow: units walk across it. Unit selection and waypoint placement can be done with either mouse or touch screen controls. The map is bigger than the screen: the arrow keys (or WASD) move the camera, and on touch screens, dragging with two fingers moves it (the touch controls wait until the fingers are lifted).

Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, soil, rock and stream bed. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. The units walk on a navigation mesh built from the terrain: slopes steeper than the "Slope max. angle" of the pathfinding behavior (22°) can't be walked on, so the units go around the hills and stay in the valley. The navigation mesh follows the terrain when it is changed. The destinations are put on the ground with the height of the terrain (the "GroundZ" expression of the terrain).

The trees, rocks and grass tufts are scattered models: each of these objects draws many copies of a 3D model on the grass of the terrain, with GPU instancing, in a few draw calls. Select one to add or erase copies with the brushes of the scene editor, or change its "Edits" property, the list of strokes painting where copies grow. The trees and the rocks have trunk collisions and the "Floor/obstacle for pathfinding (navmesh based)" behavior: the units walk around them, also after the forest is changed.

The assets are CC0: the buildings, trees, rocks and gold are from the Fantasy RTS pack by Quaternius, the textures of the ground (Grass004, Ground037, Rock023 and Ground033) are from ambientCG, and the ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt).
