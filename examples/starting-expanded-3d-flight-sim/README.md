# 3D Flight Simulator Expanded

A 3D flight game over a big landscape made with a 3D terrain: mountains with snowy tops, valleys, a lake, forests and farms. The plane is represented by a 3D model and cannot fly into the ground: it is kept above the terrain wherever it flies, and inside the map. The player controls the plane’s tilt (left/right), pitch (forward/backward), and speed using keyboard or mobile touch controls. A third-person camera follows the plane, following its angle and pitch automatically, to properly follow the player movement.

The map is big (16384 pixels wide) with a low resolution, which is enough for what is seen from the sky. Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, soil, rock and snow. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. The events use the height of the terrain under the plane (the "GroundZ" expression of the terrain) to keep it above the ground. The lake is a hollow of the terrain filled with a water object: the edges of the water are under the shore, so the shore draws the outline of the lake.

The woods are scattered models: each of these objects draws many copies of a 3D model on the grass of the terrain, with GPU instancing, in a few draw calls. Select one to add or erase copies with the brushes of the scene editor, or change its "Edits" property, the list of strokes painting where copies grow.

The assets are CC0: the barns, silo and windmill are from the Farm Buildings pack and the trees from the "Ultimate Nature" (Rocks and Trees) pack, both by Quaternius, the textures of the ground (Grass003, Ground085, Rock023 and Snow004) are from ambientCG, and the ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt).
