# 3D Flight Simulator Expanded

A 3D flight game over a large landscape made with a 3D terrain: mountains with snowy tops, valleys, a lake, forests and farms. The player flies a plane, a 3D model, by controlling its tilt (left/right), pitch (forward/backward) and speed, using keyboard or mobile touch controls. The plane stays above the ground and inside the map. A third-person camera follows the plane's angle and pitch.
