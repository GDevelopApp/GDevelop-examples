# 3D First Person Farming with a map

A 3D physics-based first-person farming game in a valley made with a 3D terrain: a farm with a barn, a silo, a windmill and fields of dirt plots, a pond, paths and hills around. The player can look around and turn using the camera, as well as jump and fall within the environment. The player can pick up objects and store them in an inventory, then place objects back onto the ground using raycasting to determine the placement location. Harvestable seed bag objects can be clicked to add seeds to the inventory, and seeds can be placed on unoccupied dirt plots to start growing crops. Fully grown crops can be picked up and added to the inventory. The inventory uses UI icons to display the contents of the inventory, as well as allow the player to select which item to use and choose which slot a picked-up object will occupy. The game supports both keyboard/mouse controls and mobile touch controls.

The fields are flat and painted with dirt, so that the plots lie on them. Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, soil, rock and sand. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. The player walks on the slopes thanks to the 3D physics behavior of the terrain, and items can be placed on the terrain as on the plots.

The trees, bushes and rocks are scattered models: each of these objects draws many copies of a 3D model on the grass of the terrain, with GPU instancing, in a few draw calls. Select one to add or erase copies with the brushes of the scene editor, or change its "Edits" property, the list of strokes painting where copies grow. The player collides with the trunks and the rocks ("Trunk collisions" and the 3D physics behavior).

The assets are CC0: the barns, silo, chicken coop, windmill, well and fences are from the Farm Buildings pack, the trees, bushes, rocks and corn from the "Ultimate Nature" (Rocks and Trees) pack, both by Quaternius, the textures of the ground (Grass004, Ground048, Rock042L and Ground054) are from ambientCG, and the ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt).
