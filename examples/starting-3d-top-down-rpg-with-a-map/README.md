# 3D RPG with a map

A 3D top-down RPG-style game with an angled down camera view, in a village by a river made with a 3D terrain, with paths between hills, forests and a bridge to cross the river. The player can move left, right, up, or down, walks up and down the slopes, but cannot walk through buildings, trees or into the river. When near an NPC, the player can press an action button to open a dialog box with text and two choice buttons. The camera follows the player along the X and Y axis without changing depth. The player character can be controlled with keyboard or touch screen controls.

Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, soil, rock and sand. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. The events put the player on the ground with the height of the terrain under it (the "GroundZ" expression of the terrain). The events also stop the player where the ground is under the water of the river: the bridge is the only way across. On the bridge, the player walks on its deck, above the river.

The trees, rocks and grass tufts are scattered models: each of these objects draws many copies of a 3D model on the grass of the terrain, with GPU instancing, in a few draw calls. Select one to add or erase copies with the brushes of the scene editor, or change its "Edits" property, the list of strokes painting where copies grow. The events push the player out of the trunks and the rocks, using the position of the nearest copy (the "NearestCopyX", "NearestCopyY" and "NearestCopyDistance" expressions of the scattered models), so this also works after the forest is changed.

The assets are CC0: the houses, inn, blacksmith, well and market stand are from the Medieval Village pack, the trees and rocks from the "Ultimate Nature" (Rocks and Trees) pack and the bridge from the Cute Fish pack, all by Quaternius, the textures of the ground (Grass004, Ground085, Rock042L and Ground054) are from ambientCG, and the ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt).
