# 3D RPG Expanded

A 3D top-down RPG-style game with an angled down camera view, in a village by a river made with a 3D terrain, with paths between hills, forests and a bridge to cross the river. The player walks up and down the slopes with keyboard or touch screen controls, and is stopped by the buildings, the trees and the river. When near an NPC, the player can press an action button to open a dialog box with text and two choice buttons. The camera follows the player.
