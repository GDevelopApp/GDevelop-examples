# 3D RPG Expanded

A 3D top-down RPG-style game with an angled down camera view, in a village by a river made with a 3D terrain, with paths between hills, forests and a bridge to cross the river. The player can move left, right, up, or down, walks up and down the slopes, but cannot walk through buildings, trees or into the river. When near an NPC, the player can press an action button to open a dialog box with text and two choice buttons. The camera follows the player along the X and Y axis without changing depth. The player character can be controlled with keyboard or touch screen controls.

Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, soil, rock and sand. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. The player is a 3D physics character (with the 3D physics behavior, the 3D character behavior, and the behaviors moving it with the keyboard and the joystick): it walks on the terrain and on the planks of the bridge, and is stopped by the static 3D physics bodies of the buildings and of the invisible walls at the limits of the map and along the river. The bridge is the only way across the river.

The trees, rocks and grass tufts are scattered models: each of these objects draws many copies of a 3D model on the grass of the terrain, with GPU instancing, in a few draw calls. Select one to add or erase copies with the brushes of the scene editor, or change its "Edits" property, the list of strokes painting where copies grow. The trees and the rocks have trunk collisions, used by the 3D physics: they stop the player, also after the forest is changed.

The assets are CC0: the houses, inn, blacksmith, well and market stand are from the Medieval Village pack, the trees and rocks from the "Ultimate Nature" (Rocks and Trees) pack and the bridge from the Cute Fish pack, all by Quaternius, the textures of the ground (Grass004, Ground085, Rock042L and Ground054) are from ambientCG, and the ripples of the water use a normal map made from "Seamless looping waves heightmaps" by zookeeper (OpenGameArt).
