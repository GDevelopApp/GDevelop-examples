# Castle courtyard (level to copy)

A walled medieval castle courtyard in 3D: corner towers, a gatehouse with a closed gate, a well, market stalls, a cart, barrels, crates, lamp posts and trees.

Made to be copied into another game: the external layout `CastleCourtyard` holds the whole level, centered on 0;0, at 50 pixels per meter. The objects are at their real size. `PlayerSpawn` marks where the player starts and the ten `KeySpot` instances mark hiding spots for collectibles.
