# 3D Grand Theft Auto

A 3D third-person physics based starter game where you can walk around, jump, get into a vehicle, drive around, and smash into cars and other obstacles, using keyboard or mobile controls. A simple starting point for creating an open-world driving and action game inspired by games like Grand Theft Auto.

The map is a **3D terrain**: a small town with streets, sidewalks, buildings and a park, a country road going through farms and climbing a hill, and mountains around it. Select the terrain in the scene editor to sculpt it (raise, lower, smooth, flatten) and paint its grass, asphalt, rock and concrete. Its shape is also described by its "Edits" property: a list of brush strokes that can be read and changed. Cars and the player collide with the ground thanks to the 3D physics behavior of the terrain.

The assets are CC0: the buildings, houses, street lights, cones, barriers and town trees are from the City Kits (Commercial, Suburban and Roads) by Kenney, the pine trees and bushes are from the "Ultimate Nature" pack by Quaternius, and the textures of the ground (Grass004, Asphalt025C, Rock058 and Concrete034) are from ambientCG.
