# 2D Light Playground

A playground for GDevelop's 2D lights and light obstacles, with one scene per
situation. Press 1, 2 or 3 to go from one scene to another.

- **Dungeon** — rooms of brick walls, where every brick is a light obstacle,
  lit by torches and a turning flashlight (a light with a texture).
- **Moving Lights and Obstacles** — coloured lights going around, crates and
  doors turning, pillars sliding: the shadows follow them. Candles next to the
  doors are hidden by them when they turn in front.
- **Crowded Room** — hundreds of crates casting shadows from many lights at
  once. The pink light follows the mouse.
