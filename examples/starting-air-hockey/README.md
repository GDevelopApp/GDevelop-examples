# Air Hockey

A top-down 2D air hockey game, where two paddles are used to hit a puck object into 2 opposing goals to score points. Players grab their paddles with either a touch on a touch screen or with a mouse on a computer, and drag their paddles around on their side of the table using physics joints and forces. The game can be played by 1 player or two players locally on the same touch screen. When 1 player is selected, the game restricts the movement of the second player paddle and controls it with a simple(but fun) enemy AI logic. With sound effects and text to show when a player has scored a point, and what the current score is.

A simple starting point for creating you own air hockey or sports game.
