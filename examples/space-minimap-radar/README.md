# Space minimap and radar

Fly a spaceship, collect the stars and reach the station, with a radar and a map of the level to find your way.

The radar (top right) turns with the ship and shows the enemies and the station. The map (bottom left) shows the whole level. Every object shown on them has the "Minimap marker" behavior: its color, shape (dot, star, square, diamond, triangle) and category are set in the behavior properties. The radar shows only the "Enemy" and "Objective" categories, and keeps the far station on its edge.
